# Privacy Policy

**Gandy**
Last updated: 2026-10-23

## Introduction

Gandy is an open-source Android application that allows you to control your Candy washing machine locally over your home Wi-Fi network. This privacy policy explains how the app handles your data.

## Data Collection

Gandy does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. The app operates entirely on your local network.

### What the app stores on your device

- **Device name and IP address**: the name you assign to your washing machine and its local IP address, stored locally on your device to connect to it.
- **Encryption key**: a device-specific key used to communicate with your washing machine, stored locally on your device.
- **Wi-Fi credentials** (during setup only): your home Wi-Fi network name and password are sent directly to the washing machine during the initial configuration process. They are not stored by the app after setup is complete.

### What the app does NOT collect

- No personal information (name, email, phone number)
- No location data
- No usage analytics or telemetry
- No advertising identifiers
- No crash reports sent to external services

## Network Communication

All network communication happens exclusively between your device and your washing machine on your local Wi-Fi network. The app does not connect to any external server, cloud service, or third-party API.

## Third-Party Services

Gandy does not integrate any third-party services, SDKs, or analytics platforms.

## Data Sharing

Since no data is collected, no data is shared with third parties.

## Data Retention

All data is stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the app removes all stored data. You can also remove individual devices from the app at any time.

## Children's Privacy

Gandy does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

Updates to this privacy policy will be posted in the app's repository. The "Last updated" date at the top indicates the most recent revision.

## Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, you can open an issue on the project's GitHub repository or contact the developer at io@nicolatomassoni.it
